# Privacy policy for Antagning.se Course Timeline

The extension helps you plan the courses you select on Antagning.se. It is independent of Antagning.se and the Swedish Council for Higher Education (UHR).

## Information the extension handles

On Antagning.se, the extension reads whether a course is selected and the course information needed for the planner. That includes its name, application code, term, credits, study pace and weeks. It also remembers whether you have opened the planner.

## Storage and sharing

The extension saves selected course information and the planner preference in the browser's site storage (`localStorage`) for the Antagning.se origin you visit. This allows the planner to work across pages and tabs of that origin in the same browser profile. The extension does not send this information to the developer or to third parties. It uses no analytics and makes no network requests of its own.

## Clearing saved information

To clear the saved course information and planner preference, clear the browser's site data for the Antagning.se origin. This can also reset other preferences for that site. If the extension remains enabled, it can read selected courses again when you visit an Antagning.se page.
